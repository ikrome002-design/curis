#### 1. **Alerts & Collaboration**




Here’s a firm breakdown of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities within the “Collaboration & Notifications” page in the Doctor’s account on the Curis platform:
________________________________________
1.	Alerts & Collaboration – MVP Functionalities


This page is the Doctor’s command center for real-time communication and workflow alerts. It ensures doctors stay in sync with clinic operations and team updates, without accessing financial or administrative controls.
________________________________________
a.	Real-Time Notifications Feed
•	Automated updates triggered by system events:
o	New appointment assigned by Receptionist or Clinic Owner
o	Upcoming consultations within a set timeframe (e.g., 15 mins, 1 hr)
o	Patient medical record updates by other staff (e.g., lab results added)
•	Notification Types:
o	Time-sensitive alerts (highlighted with urgency badges)
o	Info-only updates (standard feed)
•	Read/unread tracking
•	Clear filters: All | Unread | Appointments | Records | Consultations
________________________________________
b.	Internal Team Collaboration Panel
•	Commenting system embedded in medical records and appointments
o	Tag other Doctors or the Clinic Owner using @mention
o	Threaded replies for contextual discussions
•	Doctor-Only Discussions (not visible to patients or receptionists)
•	Time-stamped entries to track input history and decision flow
________________________________________
c.	Notification Preferences & Settings
•	Toggle alert types (email, in-app, or push if supported)
•	Define alert sensitivity (e.g., alert only for assigned patients or all team-wide changes)
•	Mute notifications during non-working hours (configurable by the Clinic Owner)
________________________________________
d.	Quick Actions via Notifications
•	Each alert includes:
o	Direct link to action (e.g., join consultation, open patient file)
o	Status indicators (e.g., "Join Now", "Awaiting Doctor Notes")
________________________________________
e.	Audit Trail & Mentions Log
•	View a log of mentions, tags, and assigned discussions
•	Helps maintain accountability and clarity in shared cases
________________________________________
This “Alerts & Collaboration” page enhances clinic productivity, ensures doctors never miss critical updates, and builds a secure communication flow without breaching role-based restrictions.
