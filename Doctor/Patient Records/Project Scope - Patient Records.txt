#### 1. **Patient Records**




Here is a clear and dynamic breakdown of the MVP features and functionalities for the "Patient Records " page within the Doctor's account on the Curis by Citrus web app:
________________________________________
1.	Patient Records  – Dynamic MVP Features (Doctor View)


a.	Patient Search & Access
•	Search bar with autocomplete for quick access to assigned patients.
•	Filter patients by name, gender, age, or visit history.
•	Option to view recently accessed patients for efficiency.
________________________________________
b.	Comprehensive Medical History View
•	Chronological display of:
o	Past consultations
o	Diagnoses
o	Prescriptions issued
o	Medical notes
•	Each entry is expandable for detailed viewing.
________________________________________
c.	Diagnosis & Clinical Notes Editor
•	Add or update:
o	Diagnoses
o	Symptoms
o	Observations
o	Treatment plans
•	Rich text editor with tagging for keywords (e.g., "chronic", "acute").
________________________________________
d.	Document & Lab Attachments
•	Upload and attach:
o	Lab results
o	Scans (PDFs, images)
o	Referral letters
•	Documents are grouped and searchable by type/date.
________________________________________
e.	Prescription Overview
•	View list of all previous prescriptions with:
o	Medication name
o	Dosage
o	Duration
o	Doctor notes
•	Quick access to re-issue or reference old prescriptions.
________________________________________
f.	Allergies & Chronic Conditions Panel
•	View and update:
o	Known allergies
o	Chronic conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension)
•	Displays as an alert on the top of the patient’s record for quick risk awareness.
________________________________________
g.	Consultation Integration
•	Quick link to start consultation if scheduled.
•	During live consultation: access this record in split-view to update notes in real-time.
________________________________________
h.	Activity Timeline & Audit Log
•	View edit history for medical records:
o	Who modified what and when.
o	Ensures accountability and tracking for compliance.
________________________________________
i.	Collaboration Comments
•	Doctors can tag other Doctors or the Clinic Owner in notes for second opinions.
•	Notifications are sent on mentions or comments.
________________________________________
j.	Security & Access Rules
•	Doctors can only access patients assigned to them.
•	No ability to delete patient records—only update fields they’re authorized to.
•	All edits logged under Doctor’s ID for traceability.
________________________________________
These dynamic features make the Patient Records  page a centralized, secure, and highly usable interface for Doctors to provide quality care and maintain updated clinical data.
