#### 1. **Consultations**




Here is a clear and direct expansion of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities within the "Consultations" page of the Doctor’s account in the Curis by Citrus SaaS platform:
________________________________________
1.	Consultations Hub – MVP Functional Overview


The Consultations page serves as the Doctor’s centralized interface for managing and conducting both in-person and online consultations, integrating real-time access to patient data and seamless communication.
________________________________________
Core Functionalities


a.	Consultation List View
•	Displays a structured list of all consultations:
o	Upcoming, Ongoing, Completed, and Missed
•	Each entry shows:
o	Patient name & photo
o	Scheduled date/time
o	Consultation mode (Online via Google Meet / In-clinic)
o	Status badge (e.g., Scheduled, Ongoing, Completed, Missed)
•	Supports search and filters by:
o	Date range
o	Patient name
o	Status
________________________________________
b.	Start/Join Consultation
•	For online appointments:
o	"Join Consultation" button opens a Google Meet session in a new tab.
•	For in-clinic:
o	"Start Consultation" logs the session as active for tracking.
________________________________________
c.	Access to Medical Records
•	While in a consultation, doctor can:
o	Instantly view patient’s medical history, allergies, past prescriptions, chronic conditions, and previous visit notes.
o	No need to switch tabs—data opens in a side drawer or modal.
________________________________________
d.	Real-time Notes & Observations
•	Live editable note-taking during the session:
o	Diagnosis
o	Observations
o	Vitals
o	Next steps
•	Notes are autosaved and directly linked to the patient’s record.
________________________________________
e.	Post-Consultation Summary
•	Upon ending a session, doctor can:
o	Review notes
o	Mark the consultation as Completed
o	Trigger follow-up creation or send prescription if needed
o	Attach related documents or lab results
________________________________________
f.	Alerts & Reminders
•	Smart reminders show:
o	Countdown to upcoming consultations
o	Notifications for missed consultations
o	Alerts when a patient joins an online session
________________________________________
Role-Specific Limitations


•	Doctor can only view/manage consultations assigned to them.
•	Cannot reassign or cancel consultations (Clinic Owner or Receptionist has control).
•	Consultation records are audit-locked once marked complete—can only be edited with permission from Clinic Owner.
________________________________________
This page ensures that doctors can seamlessly deliver consultations—both virtual and in-person—while having all relevant medical context and documentation tools at their fingertips, without breaching access boundaries set by the system.
