#### 1. **Prescriptions**




Here’s a firm and detailed breakdown of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities within the "Prescriptions" page of the Doctor’s account in the Curis by Citrus SaaS platform:
________________________________________
1.	Prescriptions Page – MVP Functional Overview


The Prescriptions page is a dedicated interface that allows the doctor to issue accurate, trackable, and structured digital prescriptions that automatically integrate into the patient’s record and portal.
________________________________________
Core Functionalities

a.	Prescription Creation Interface
•	Doctor initiates a new prescription linked to:
o	A specific patient
o	A specific appointment or consultation (auto-linked if session is active)
•	Form includes:
o	Medication name (dropdown or autocomplete from clinic-defined medication database)
o	Dosage (e.g., 500mg, 2 tablets)
o	Frequency (e.g., once daily, every 8 hours)
o	Duration (e.g., 5 days)
o	Route of administration (oral, topical, injection)
o	Additional instructions (free text)
________________________________________
b.	Medication Database Integration
•	Doctors select drugs from a centralized clinic-defined medication list, ensuring consistency and preventing unauthorized entries.
•	Autocomplete assists quick input with suggested doses.
________________________________________
c.	Digital Prescription Preview & Confirmation
•	Doctors can preview prescription before issuing.
•	Edits can be made until submission.
•	Once confirmed:
o	The prescription is time-stamped, doctor-signed, and stored in the patient’s record.
________________________________________
d.	Auto-Sync to Patient Portal
•	Once issued:
o	The digital prescription is automatically pushed to the Patient Portal.
o	Patients can view and download their prescription in PDF format.
o	Optional: Include QR code or prescription ID for pharmacy verification.
________________________________________
e.	Prescriptions History Log
•	Doctors can:
o	View a list of all past prescriptions issued to each patient.
o	Click to view details.
o	Re-issue or update based on follow-up consultations.
________________________________________
f.	Security & Integrity
•	Doctors cannot delete prescriptions once submitted.
•	Edits require supervisor (Clinic Owner) authorization.
•	System ensures non-redundant entries (e.g., blocking duplicate active prescriptions unless justified).
________________________________________
Access Control

•	Doctors can only view and issue prescriptions for patients assigned to them.
•	Cannot access prescriptions created by other doctors.
•	Prescription data is immutable after submission to meet compliance standards.
________________________________________
This Prescriptions page ensures accurate medication management, secure record-keeping, and immediate patient access—all within the controlled framework defined by the Clinic Owner.
