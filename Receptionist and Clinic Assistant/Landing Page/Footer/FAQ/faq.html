General Information

Curis is a role-based clinic management platform developed by Citrus Labs. It helps receptionists and clinic assistants efficiently handle patient check-ins, appointment bookings, billing, and patient communication.

Curis runs best on modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) with stable internet. For mobile, use Android 9+ or iOS 12+ for optimal performance.

Your clinic admin will create your user account. Once you receive login credentials, access the platform at [Curis URL], set your password, and start managing clinic operations.

Account Management

Receptionist and Clinic Assistant accounts have access to scheduling, billing, and patient records but cannot manage platform settings or view admin reports.

Go to your profile menu → "My Account" → Edit your name, phone, or email. Changes are saved immediately.

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email and follow the reset link sent to you.

Appointments & Scheduling

Click on the calendar or "Book Appointment" tab, select the patient, choose a service and time slot, and confirm.

Yes. Find the appointment in the calendar, click "Edit" or "Cancel," and follow the prompts.

During or after check-in, you can add notes under "Visit Summary" for staff reference. Notes are timestamped and saved securely.

Billing & Payments

After a visit is marked complete, click "Generate Invoice," enter services rendered, and save. The system auto-calculates totals.

Under the "Billing" tab, check the status of invoices (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid) and view payment history.

Curis supports M-Pesa till numbers. Patients send payments, and the system verifies them automatically. A confirmation message appears once validated.

Patient Communication

Curis sends SMS alerts for upcoming appointments, cancellations, or reschedules, based on each patient's preferences.

Yes. Email notices are sent for appointment confirmations and invoices if the patient's email is on file.

Edit their record under "Patient Info" → "Notifications." You can enable/disable SMS and email alerts as needed.
