#### 1. **Patient Directory**




Here is the dynamic MVP feature breakdown for the "Patient Directory" page within the Receptionists & Clinic Assistants' Curis by Citrus web app account:
________________________________________
Page Title: Patient Directory
________________________________________
Core Objective:
Enable receptionists and clinic assistants to efficiently identify, reference, and manage basic patient data—without exposing any sensitive medical or clinical records.
________________________________________
Dynamic MVP Features & Functionalities:

a.	Patient List View (Searchable & Paginated)
•	Display a real-time list of all registered patients linked to the clinic.
•	Search bar with smart filters: name, contact number, visit frequency, last appointment date.
•	Pagination and sorting by Name | Last Visit | Registration Date.
b.	Patient Profile Preview (Read-Only View)
On selecting a patient from the list:
•	Name & Gender
•	Mobile Number & Email (if provided)
•	Date of Birth (optional)
•	Registration Date
•	Profile photo/avatar (if available)
•	Appointment history: past and upcoming visits (doctor, date, time)
c.	Appointment History Timeline
•	Dynamic timeline or tabbed list showing all past and scheduled appointments:
o	Doctor’s name (clickable to view availability)
o	Appointment date and time
o	Status: Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled / No-show
o	Booked via: Reception / Patient App
d.	Quick Actions Panel
•	Book New Appointment (auto-fills patient info into scheduling module)
•	Resend Appointment Reminder (SMS/Email/WhatsApp)
•	Update Contact Info (editable form for phone/email with audit logs)
e.	Audit-Protected Access
•	Every user action (viewing, editing contact info, etc.) is logged and timestamped for accountability.
•	No ability to export, print, or download patient profiles—read-only within platform.
________________________________________
Strict Access Controls

Receptionists & Assistants cannot:
•	View or edit diagnosis, clinical notes, prescriptions, lab results, or attachments.
•	Access mental health history, medical forms, or any records uploaded by doctors.
________________________________________
Use Cases

•	Confirming identity of walk-in patients.
•	Verifying contact details before sending reminders or receipts.
•	Referencing last visit date when scheduling a follow-up.
