#### 1. **Inbox & Alerts**




1. Inbox & Alerts – Dynamic MVP Features & Functionalities
(Receptionist & Clinic Assistant Account — Curis by Citrus)


The Inbox & Alerts page is the central hub for real-time communication and operational notifications. It empowers Receptionists and Clinic Assistants to stay updated, coordinate smoothly, and ensure timely responses to clinic events without accessing sensitive patient data. Below is a breakdown of its core dynamic MVP functionalities:
________________________________________
a.	Real-Time Alerts Feed
•	Live Appointment Notifications
o	Instant alerts for new bookings, reschedules, cancellations, and late arrivals.
o	Notifications are tagged by urgency: [Normal], [High] (e.g., walk-in emergency), and [System Alert] (e.g., service interruptions).
•	Emergency Broadcasts
o	High-priority updates from Clinic Owner or system admin (e.g., doctor unavailable, system outage, fire drill).
•	Patient Arrival Alerts
o	Auto-prompted notification when a patient completes self-check-in or is manually checked in by staff.
________________________________________
b.	Internal Messaging (Clinic Chat)
•	Role-Based Messaging Threads
o	Chat groups segmented by roles: Reception–Doctor, Reception–Lab, Reception–Clinic Owner.
o	1-on-1 direct chats between staff for quick coordination.
•	Contextual Smart Replies
o	One-tap response suggestions for common tasks (e.g., “Patient is ready”, “Rescheduled for 3PM”).
•	Attachment Support
o	Send internal notes, documents (e.g., appointment schedules, patient status), and system-generated links (e.g., invoice or task references).
•	Read Receipts & Timestamps
o	Tracks when messages are delivered and read for accountability and workflow visibility.
________________________________________
c.	Patient Notification Panel
•	Outbound Announcements
o	Send bulk or individual appointment reminders, confirmations, and delay notifications via:
	SMS
	WhatsApp
	Email
•	Broadcast Messages
o	Clinic-wide updates (e.g., holiday closures, new services) configured and sent under Clinic Owner guidance.
•	Template Library
o	Pre-set message templates editable for use cases such as:
	“Your appointment is confirmed for [Time] with Dr. [Name].”
	“Clinic is temporarily closed today due to [Reason].”
________________________________________
d.	Alerts History Log
•	Notification Archive
o	Track past alerts and messages.
o	Filterable by:
	Date
	Type (Appointment, Emergency, System)
	Sender
•	Audit Trail
o	Displays who sent or responded to a notification, ensuring traceability and accountability.
________________________________________
e.	Smart Filter & Prioritization Tools
•	Alert Filters
o	Quickly sort alerts by type, time, or read/unread status.
•	Urgency Tags & Color Codes
o	High-priority items are visually highlighted (e.g., red banner for emergencies, blue for confirmations).
•	Snooze & Follow-Up Reminders
o	Temporarily snooze non-urgent alerts.
o	Set follow-up notifications (e.g., remind me in 30 minutes to call the patient).
________________________________________
Value Delivered

•	Real-time visibility into front-desk operations and clinic events.
•	Streamlined coordination across clinic staff without the need for external apps.
•	Timely patient communication enhances service experience and reduces no-shows.
•	Accountability through audit logs and role-based access.
