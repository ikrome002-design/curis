#### 1. **Appointment**




Here is a firm and direct breakdown of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities within the "Appointments" page of the Receptionist & Clinic Assistant account on the Curis by Citrus platform:
________________________________________
2. APPOINTMENTS – Dynamic MVP Page Functionalities

This page serves as the operational hub for managing patient scheduling and streamlining clinic flow without requiring access to confidential medical records.
________________________________________
a.	Appointment Listings (Upcoming & Past)
•	Live search and filter options (by patient name, doctor, date, time, status).
•	Sort by appointment date, status, or doctor for quick access.
•	Color-coded status tags (e.g., Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, Completed).
________________________________________
b.	Booking Appointments
•	Interactive calendar view for doctor availability.
•	Smart form to book new appointments:
o	Select patient (existing or register new).
o	Choose doctor based on available time slots.
o	Input reason for visit (optional).
o	Auto-suggest next available slots.
________________________________________
c.	Confirming, Rescheduling, or Cancelling Appointments
•	One-click confirmation toggle with SMS/email confirmation sent to patient.
•	Drag-and-drop rescheduling in calendar or use dropdown time-picker.
•	Cancellation options include reason input and automatic patient notification.
________________________________________
d.	Doctor Availability Management
•	View real-time doctor schedules.
•	Highlight double-booking conflicts.
•	Display off-duty/unavailable time slots (read-only, set by Clinic Owner/Doctor).
________________________________________
e.	Automated Reminders System
•	Auto-triggered SMS/email reminders:
o	24 hours and 1 hour before appointments.
o	Custom reminders for specific services if enabled by clinic.
•	Retry logic for failed reminders.
________________________________________
f.	Conflict Detection & Resolution
•	Warn users of schedule overlaps or unavailable slots.
•	Suggest nearest alternative slots.
•	Option to escalate unresolvable conflicts to Clinic Owner.
________________________________________
g.	Audit Trail & Logs
•	Each appointment action (create, edit, cancel) is timestamped and user-logged.
•	Logs available to Clinic Owner for oversight.
________________________________________
h.	Real-Time Notifications
•	System notifies Receptionist:
o	When new bookings are made by patients (if enabled).
o	When patients confirm or cancel from their end.
o	If doctors manually update their schedules.
________________________________________
i.	Patient Communication Tools
•	Send appointment info/reminders via:
o	In-app alerts
o	WhatsApp (if configured)
o	SMS or email
•	Predefined message templates for speed and consistency.
________________________________________
Key Outcome


This page equips receptionists with a centralized, efficient, and audit-secure interface to manage patient appointments accurately, optimize scheduling, and ensure timely communication with patients and doctors—while remaining within their permitted access boundaries.
