#### 1. **Dashboard**




Here’s an expanded breakdown of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities available within the “Dashboard” page for the Receptionists & Clinic Assistants account on the Curis by Citrus platform:
________________________________________
1.	Dashboard – Dynamic Features Overview

The Dashboard acts as the centralized command center for Receptionists and Clinic Assistants, providing a real-time, role-specific interface to streamline front-desk operations. It is designed for efficiency, situational awareness, and proactive clinic management.
________________________________________
a.	Quick Access to Appointments
•	Real-time display of upcoming and past appointments.
•	Visual indicators (color-coded) show appointment status: confirmed, pending, cancelled, or completed.
•	Instant actions: "Check-in Patient," "Reschedule," "Cancel," or "Assign to Doctor" with one click.
________________________________________
b.	Daily Task Summary
•	Auto-generated checklist of key responsibilities for the day:
o	Scheduled patient check-ins
o	Pending appointment confirmations
o	Walk-in registration reminders
o	Unpaid invoices requiring follow-up
•	Tasks update dynamically based on real-time user actions and system events.
________________________________________
c.	Real-Time Notifications
•	Pop-up and banner alerts for:
o	New bookings or reschedules made by patients or doctors
o	Cancellations and no-shows
o	Patient arrivals and waiting status updates
o	Billing events, such as payment received or overdue invoice flagged
•	Customizable notification settings per user session.
________________________________________
d.	Doctor Availability Status
•	Live feed of doctor schedules with current availability indicators (e.g., “In Consultation,” “Available,” “On Break”).
•	Hover or tap to view each doctor’s appointment queue.
•	Used to assign incoming patients or reschedule based on real-time availability.
________________________________________
e.	Performance Metrics
•	Visual counters and charts display daily stats:
o	Patients checked in today
o	Total appointments handled
o	Completed tasks vs pending
o	Payment collection tally (non-sensitive overview)
•	Supports accountability and helps front-desk staff track productivity in real time.
________________________________________
f.	System Shortcuts & Quick Actions Panel
•	Direct links to frequently used pages:
o	Book Appointment,
o	Check-in Patient,
o	View Queue,
o	Create Invoice,
o	Send Payment Reminder
•	Search bar with smart suggestions for patients, doctors, or invoices.
________________________________________
g.	Alerts & Flags Section
•	Displays alerts such as:
o	Overdue patient bills
o	Unassigned appointments
o	Service fee invoice due (for clinic operations)
•	Clicking on each alert leads directly to the relevant action screen.
________________________________________
h.	Integrated Messaging Notifications
•	Notification preview for unread internal messages from doctors or staff.
•	Quick access to the secure messaging portal for coordination.
________________________________________
Role-Specific Control

All Dashboard data and interactions are scoped strictly to the receptionist/clinic assistant role:
•	No access to patient medical records, diagnoses, or doctor analytics.
•	View-limited financial summaries tied to front-desk actions (e.g., invoices and receipts handled by the assistant).
________________________________________
Purpose

The Dashboard is the receptionist’s operational hub—providing full visibility into the day’s flow, minimizing oversight, and enabling fast decision-making without needing to navigate multiple tabs or screens.
