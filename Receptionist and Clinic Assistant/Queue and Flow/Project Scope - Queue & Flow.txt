#### 1. **Queue & Flow**




Dynamic MVP Features and Functionalities – “Queue & Flow” Page
Receptionist/Clinic Assistant Account – Curis by Citrus
The “Queue & Flow” page is a real-time operational hub for Receptionists and Clinic Assistants to actively manage patient flow, reduce wait times, and improve clinic efficiency—without touching clinical data.
________________________________________
a.	Live Queue Overview
•	Real-Time Patient Queue Display:
Displays all patients currently in queue, their status (e.g., “Waiting,” “In Progress,” “Complete”), appointment time, and assigned doctor.
•	Color-Coded Status Indicators:
Visual cues for wait stages—e.g., green (checked-in), amber (delayed), red (overdue), blue (being seen).
________________________________________
b.	Dynamic Wait Time Tracking
•	Individual Patient Wait Timers:
Automatically starts a timer once a patient checks in—tracks exact wait duration.
•	Alerts for Excessive Wait Times:
System flags patients exceeding the average or set threshold wait time.
________________________________________
c.	Queue Actions & Controls
•	Reorder Queue Position:
Manually drag-and-drop patients (e.g., urgent walk-ins prioritized with doctor consent).
•	Mark Patient as “Seen” / “No Show” / “Deferred”:
Helps update queue in real-time with relevant follow-up options.
•	Assign or Reassign Doctor:
Allows the Receptionist to quickly reassign a patient to an available doctor in case of scheduling delays or absences.
________________________________________
d.	Doctor Status & Sync
•	Live Doctor Availability Feed:
Displays which doctors are actively consulting, idle, or unavailable—sourced from the Dashboard and Appointment Management module.
•	Consultation Room Readiness Indicator:
Visual status showing which rooms/doctors are “Ready for Next Patient” or “Occupied.”
________________________________________
e.	Patient Notifications Integration
•	Trigger In-App & SMS Alerts:
Send instant messages to patients (e.g., “You’re next,” “Doctor delayed by 10 minutes”).
•	Pre-Consultation Instructions:
Attach custom messages (e.g., "Have your ID ready" or "Kindly sanitize before entry").
________________________________________
f.	Queue Analytics & Flow Metrics
•	Live Queue Volume Counter:
Tracks how many patients are in queue per doctor or room.
•	Average Wait Time Monitor:
Auto-calculated and updated in real-time to identify bottlenecks.
________________________________________
g.	Multi-Doctor Queue Segmentation
•	Separate Queues per Doctor or Department:
Enables the receptionist to manage independent queues, especially in multi-specialty clinics.
•	Merge or Split Queues:
Temporarily combine queues (e.g., during doctor absence) or revert when full staff resumes.
________________________________________
h.	Audit & Logging
•	All Queue Movements Logged:
Every patient queue status change is audit-logged with timestamp, receptionist ID, and action taken.
________________________________________
Summary of Value Delivered:

•	Enables proactive patient flow control without accessing medical data.
•	Reduces patient frustration by improving transparency and communication.
•	Ensures coordination with doctors and adherence to scheduled consultations.
•	Centralizes check-in, wait tracking, and queue adjustments into a single interface.
