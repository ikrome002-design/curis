General Platform Info

What is Curis?

Curis is a digital platform designed to simplify clinic operations. It offers tools for patient management, invoicing, staff oversight, and seamless billing—all under one unified dashboard.

What are the system requirements to use Curis?

Curis works on any modern web browser with a stable internet connection. No downloads or installations are required.

How do I get started?

Create your account using the sign-up form. Once registered, you'll gain access to the clinic owner dashboard where you can begin adding staff, patients, and managing operations.

Account Management

What user roles are supported?

Curis supports Clinic Owners, Admin Staff, and Medical Practitioners. Each role has specific permissions tailored for their tasks.

How do I manage my profile settings?

From your dashboard, go to 'Profile Settings' to update personal and clinic information.

Can I add staff members to my account?

Yes. Use the 'Staff Management' section to invite and assign roles to your clinic team.

Patient Management

How do I check in a new or returning patient?

Use the 'Patient Check-In' section to register new patients or locate and update existing patient records.

Where are treatment notes stored?

All notes are securely stored in the patient's profile under 'Treatment Notes & History'.

Can I upload patient documents or photos?

Yes. Use the 'Document Uploads' feature within each patient's profile.

How does the patient consent process work?

Curis includes a digital consent workflow that ensures all necessary forms are reviewed and signed before treatment begins.

Invoicing & Payments

How do I create an invoice?

Go to 'Creating Invoices' in the dashboard, select the patient, input services, and the system will auto-calculate totals including the 15% service charge.

How is the service fee calculated?

Curis automatically adds a 15% service charge on the total invoice amount.

How do I send invoices to patients?

Invoices can be sent directly via SMS or email from within the platform.

Can I track invoice payments?

Yes. Use the 'Payment Tracking' tool to monitor pending, paid, and overdue invoices.

Billing & Service Charges

How does Curis generate revenue?

Curis operates on a pay-as-you-earn model. A 15% service charge is applied per invoice.

Who is responsible for paying the 15% service fee?

The Clinic Owner is responsible for collecting and remitting the 15% service charge to Curis.

How do I remit the service charges?

Payments must be sent via M-Pesa to the official Curis Till Number displayed on your dashboard.

Are there any monthly subscription fees?

No. Curis does not charge any monthly or annual subscription fees.

What happens if I delay service charge payment?

Delays in remitting service charges may result in limited access to platform features or account suspension.

Where can I get help with billing issues?

Use the 'Billing Support' section or contact support directly through your dashboard.

Support Resources

How can I contact Curis support?

Use the 'Contact Support' form in your dashboard or call the support line provided.

Are there video tutorials available?

Yes. Visit the 'Video Tutorials' section for step-by-step guides on using every feature.

How do I stay updated with changes?

Follow the 'System Updates' section for news on feature rollouts and platform maintenance.
