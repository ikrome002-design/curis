Curis Data Policy – Your Trusted Data Governance for Clinic Owners

Effective Date: January 11th, 2025

Last Updated: March 21st, 2025

At Curis, powered by Citrus Labs Limited, we understand that as a Clinic Owner, safeguarding patient and operational data is paramount. Our Data Policy is meticulously designed not only to meet, but exceed the stringent requirements of Kenya's data protection laws in 2025. Here's how we deliver a robust and transparent data management framework for your clinic:

1. Introduction & Transparency

Our Data Policy sets the foundation for a secure and compliant digital ecosystem.

About Curis:

Curis is an all-in-one clinic management platform that streamlines operations and enhances patient care. We are committed to clear, transparent practices.

Scope & Definitions:

Our policy covers the collection, use, and protection of all data processed through Curis—whether it's patient records, staff information, or payment details—ensuring every term is clearly defined and understood.

2. Data Collection – Comprehensive Yet Respectful

We collect essential data needed to deliver superior clinic management services:

Patient Data:

This includes personal information, contact details, medical records, and notification preferences to help you provide tailored patient care.

Personal Information

Basic identifying data that helps track patient records accurately.

Contact Details

Information needed to reach patients regarding appointments and care.

Medical Records

Secure storage of patient health information and treatment history.

Notification Preferences

Patient settings for how they wish to receive communications.

Staff Data:

Manage your clinic's human resources with secure storage of staff account information and activity logs.

Staff Account Information

Professional credentials and access level information for clinic staff.

Access & Activity Logs

Records of system access and actions performed for security and accountability.

Payment Data:

Detailed invoice and transaction records ensure accurate billing and financial reporting.

Invoice Information

Details of services rendered and billing information.

Transaction History

Secure records of all financial transactions processed through the platform.

Every piece of data is collected responsibly and only to enhance service delivery, adhering strictly to the principle of data minimization.

3. Data Use – Enhancing Your Service Delivery

At Curis, data is a tool for empowerment, not risk:

Service Delivery & Personalization:

We use your data to streamline appointment scheduling, deliver personalized platform experiences, and continuously improve service quality.

Analytics & Communications:

Data insights drive our performance reports and help us communicate important updates through secure email and SMS notifications.

Email Communications

Secure delivery of important updates and notifications.

SMS Notifications

Time-sensitive alerts delivered directly to mobile devices.

Data Sharing with Partners

Controlled sharing of relevant information with trusted partners.

Partner Labs

Secure transfer of test orders and results with laboratory partners.

Partner Pharmacies

Streamlined prescription fulfillment with pharmacy networks.

Legal Authorities

Compliance with mandatory reporting requirements to relevant authorities.

4. Data Security – Your Data, Fully Protected

Robust security measures protect your clinic's sensitive information:

Storage & Encryption:

Data is stored using advanced, industry-standard methods with full encryption to secure all information.

Access Controls & Monitoring:

Our system uses strict role-based access controls and continuous monitoring to guarantee that only authorized users can access sensitive data.

Role-Based Access

Access privileges granted based on specific user roles and responsibilities.

Authentication Methods

Multi-factor authentication and secure login protocols.

System Monitoring

Continuous surveillance of system activity to detect unauthorized access attempts.

Retention & Breach Protocol:

Data is retained only as long as necessary, and in the event of a breach, our rapid response protocols ensure immediate notification and mitigation.

Active Data

How we manage and protect currently active data records.

Archived/Inactivated Data

Secure storage of historical records no longer in active use.

Data Deletion Process

Systematic procedures for permanently removing data when requested or required.

Your rights, and those of your patients, are central to our data practices:

Access & Correction:

Easily request access to, and correction of, data we hold on your behalf.

How to Request Data

Simple process for submitting data access requests.

Response Timeline

Expected timeframes for our response to your data requests.

Deletion & Restriction:

Exercise your right to delete your data or restrict how it is processed.

Portability:

6. Compliance – Adhering to Kenyan Law & International Standards

Curis is fully committed to upholding data protection standards:

Local Laws & Standards:

Our practices comply with all relevant Kenyan data protection laws, ensuring that your clinic's data is managed in accordance with the highest local legal requirements.

Certifications & Audits:

We maintain certifications for data security and conduct regular internal audits, guaranteeing our ongoing commitment to excellence and legal compliance.
