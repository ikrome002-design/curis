1. Introduction

Who We Are:

Curis is a clinic management platform brought to you by Citrus Labs Limited. We are dedicated to protecting the privacy and security of your information, setting the industry standard under Kenya's 2025 legal requirements.

Scope of Policy:

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, sharing, and protection of your information as a clinic owner. It applies to all data handled by our system and clarifies your rights and obligations.

Consent to Policy:

By using Curis, you expressly consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms, as required by Kenya's statutory privacy laws.

Key Terms:

For clarity, we define "personal information," "data," "processing," "consent," and other critical concepts to ensure you understand your rights and our obligations.

  • Account Details

  • Staff Information

    Essential data about authorized staff is collected to ensure proper role management.

  • Clinic Contact Details

    We maintain up-to-date contact information for all communication.

Patient Information:

  • Personal Identifiers

    This includes names, national identification, and other identifying information.

  • Contact Information

    We collect telephone numbers, addresses, and email data for essential communication.

  • Health Records

    Comprehensive, confidential health records are stored in compliance with healthcare regulations.

  • Communication Preferences

    Patient choices regarding communication channels are recorded to tailor service delivery.

Payment Information:

  • Transaction Records

    Secure logs of every transaction processed through Curis.

  • Invoice Data

    Detailed information on invoices generated and sent for billing purposes.

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device Information

    Technical details about the devices used to access our platform.

    Data logs to help monitor and maintain system performance.

    How you and your staff interact with Curis for continuous service improvement.

  • Cookies & Similar Technologies

    Automatically collected data to improve user experience and track platform usage.

3. How We Use Your Information

Service Provision & Improvement:

We use your data to deliver, maintain, and enhance the services offered by Curis. Our goal is to keep your clinic operations running smoothly and efficiently.

Communications & Updates:

Your information is used to send necessary notifications, legal updates, and important communications about your account and regulatory changes.

Analytics & Reporting:

Data is analyzed to generate insights that help optimize our service, ensuring that we meet both your needs and legal obligations.

Legal Obligations:

We process your information in accordance with Kenyan law, meeting all statutory requirements and obligations.

4. Information Sharing

Service Providers:

Legal Requirements:

We may disclose data as mandated by law or in response to legal requests by regulatory authorities.

With Consent:

5. Data Security

Security Measures:

Curis implements advanced technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Data Storage:

Your data is stored securely in a manner that meets or exceeds Kenya's regulatory requirements.

Data Retention:
