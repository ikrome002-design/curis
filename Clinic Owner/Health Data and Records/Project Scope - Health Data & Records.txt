CLINIC OWNER - CURIS.


---

For the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of the **Health Data & Records** page within the **clinic account user** in *Curis by Citrus*, the focus is on providing structured tools that enable clinic owners to efficiently handle patient information, maintain medical histories, and ensure secure record-keeping for seamless healthcare delivery. This page is designed to facilitate organized patient data access while supporting operational efficiency and compliance with medical record standards. Here's a breakdown of the essential features available on this management page:  

________________________________________  
### **Core MVP Features of the Health Data & Records Page**


Based on the outlined MVP structure for the Clinic Owner account in the Curis by Citrus platform, the "Health Data & Records" page within the MVP plays a foundational but dynamic role in enabling clinical efficiency and oversight. Here's an expanded breakdown of the dynamic features and functionalities of this section:
________________________________________
1. Patient Registration & Profile Management


•	Manual Registration: Clinic Owner and authorized staff (e.g., Receptionist) can register new patients using a structured input form.
•	Profile Fields Include:
o	Full Name, Phone Number, Email
o	Gender, Date of Birth, ID/Passport Number
o	Emergency Contact Details
o	Insurance Details (if applicable)
•	Editable Basic Info: Patients can log in and update basic details (e.g., phone, email, address) without compromising medical data integrity.

________________________________________
2. Centralized Patient Directory


•	Searchable List View of all registered patients by name, phone number, or patient ID.
•	Filter & Sort Options:
o	By last visit date, registration date, gender, or assigned doctor.
•	Quick Actions: View patient profile, appointment history, or billing summary from the directory.

________________________________________
3. Medical History Access (High-Level Oversight)


•	View-Only Access to Medical Records:
o	Clinic Owner can see structured metadata such as:
	Visit summaries
	Diagnoses
	Lab test types (not results)
	Medications prescribed (not dosage instructions)
o	No access to sensitive doctor notes or private consultations to maintain patient-doctor confidentiality.
•	Record Timeline View:
o	Chronological overview of medical interactions (appointments, procedures, prescriptions).

________________________________________
4. Appointment & Billing Linkage


•	For each patient, view:
o	Appointment History – Status, date, assigned staff, feedback.
o	Invoices & Payments – Services availed, invoice totals, payment status (paid, unpaid, partially paid).

________________________________________
5. Patient Activity Monitoring


•	Last Seen/Active timestamps
•	Track number of visits, cancellations, and reschedules
•	Feedback history and ratings (if enabled)

________________________________________
6. Patient Self-Service Sync


•	Enable patients to:
o	Update their contact details securely.
o	View their medical summary and appointment records.
o	Download their billing/invoice history.

________________________________________
7. Staff-Patient Relationship Overview


•	See which Doctor or Receptionist has handled specific patients.
•	Identify patient load per staff member for workload balancing.

________________________________________
8. Export & Data Portability


•	Clinic Owner can export filtered patient records (non-sensitive data) in CSV or PDF format for reporting or backup.

________________________________________
Admin & Compliance Layer

•	Audit Trail: Every modification or view of a patient record is logged and tied to the user who accessed or modified it.
•	Role-Based Access Control:
o	Only Clinic Owners and authorized staff can access patient profiles.
o	Medical record visibility follows strict hierarchy: Clinic Owner (high-level only), Doctor (full access), Receptionist (limited view).
________________________________________

This dynamic Health Data & Records module ensures Clinic Owners maintain full administrative oversight without breaching clinical privacy, enabling streamlined workflows, better continuity of care, and real-time access to operational data tied to each patient.
