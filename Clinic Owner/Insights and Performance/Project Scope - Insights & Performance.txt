CLINIC OWNER - CURIS.


---

For the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of the **Insights & Performance** page within the **clinic account user** in *Curis by Citrus*, the focus is on equipping clinic owners with powerful data-driven insights, financial analytics, and operational performance metrics to enhance decision-making and clinic efficiency. This page provides an overview of revenue trends, service utilization, staff performance, and patient engagement, ensuring transparent clinic oversight and continuous improvement. Here's a breakdown of the essential features available on this analytics page:  

________________________________________  
### **Core MVP Features of the Insights & Performance Page**


The “Insights & Performance” page within the Clinic Owner's Curis by Citrus MVP account is a central analytics hub, providing data-driven insights to manage clinic operations, revenue, patient engagement, and staff performance. Below is a direct expansion of its dynamic MVP features and functionalities:
________________________________________
1. Financial Performance Reports


•	Total Income Overview:
o	Aggregated revenue from services (consultations, labs, procedures) over selectable timeframes (daily, weekly, monthly).
•	Service Charge Deductions:
o	Automated display of Curis platform deductions per payment frequency (e.g., weekly 10% deductions).
•	Outstanding Balances:
o	Filterable report showing unpaid invoices by patient name, service date, and amount due.
•	Revenue by Service Type:
o	Breakdown of earnings per service category (e.g., General Consultation, Blood Test, Minor Surgery).
•	Downloadable Reports:
o	Export financial summaries in CSV or PDF for offline bookkeeping or audit.

________________________________________
2. Appointment Analytics


•	Booking Trends:
o	Volume of appointments per day/week/month.
o	Identify peak patient traffic days/times.
•	Cancellation & Reschedule Metrics:
o	Track appointment drop-offs and changes, including reasons logged by staff or patients.
•	No-Show Rates:
o	Percentage of patients who missed appointments without prior notice, aiding in overbooking or follow-up planning.
•	Utilization Rate:
o	Ratio of booked slots to available slots across the clinic’s schedule.

________________________________________
3. Clinic Service Performance


•	Patient Retention Tracking:
o	Percentage of returning patients versus new patients over a period.
•	Service Utilization:
o	Top 5 most and least used services/specialties.
•	Revenue vs Utilization Correlation:
o	Match services with high earnings but low usage, guiding promotional or pricing strategies.

________________________________________
4. Staff Efficiency Metrics


•	Appointments Handled:
o	Number of consultations handled by each Doctor or Receptionist.
•	Service Ratings:
o	Aggregated patient feedback per staff member based on post-service surveys (e.g., 1–5 stars or emoji scale).
•	Response Times:
o	Time taken to confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments by staff.
•	Action Logs:
o	Overview of staff-initiated actions (e.g., created invoice, edited appointment, updated patient file).

________________________________________
5. Visualized Dashboards


•	Graphs and Charts:
o	Line graphs for income trends, bar charts for staff performance, pie charts for service usage.
•	Filter Tools:
o	Date range picker, staff/service filters, patient segments (new vs returning).
•	Comparative Views:
o	Compare current period vs previous (e.g., this month vs last month).

________________________________________
6. Report Export & Sharing


•	Export Options:
o	PDF (summary snapshots), CSV (raw data tables).
•	Scheduled Report Delivery (optional future upgrade):
o	Email weekly or monthly reports automatically to the Clinic Owner’s registered email.

________________________________________
Summary Utility of This Page


•	Insight-Driven Decisions: Enables data-backed staffing, marketing, and pricing strategies.
•	Operational Visibility: Tracks how each part of the clinic ecosystem is performing.
•	Financial Governance: Monitors earnings, costs, and profitability in real time.
•	Staff Accountability: Offers performance benchmarks and evidence for rewards or corrective measures.
