CLINIC OWNER - CURIS.


---

For the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of the **Billings & Payments** page within the **clinic account user** in *Curis by Citrus*, the focus is on providing intuitive financial management tools that enable clinic owners to track revenue, handle invoices, process payments, and manage service charge deductions. This page ensures transparent financial oversight while streamlining billing operations for efficient clinic management. Here's a breakdown of the essential features available on this billings page:  

________________________________________  
### **Core MVP Features of the Billings & Payments Page**



This module gives Clinic Owners full visibility and control over financial operations, ensuring every service rendered is properly billed and tracked, while also monitoring Curis’ platform service fees.

________________________________________
1. Invoice Generation & Tracking

•	Dynamically auto-generate invoices immediately after service completion, tagged to:
o	Patient name
o	Doctor who attended
o	Type of service rendered
o	Date and time
•	Option for manual invoice creation by Clinic Owner or Receptionist for ad-hoc services.
•	Invoices show line-item service breakdowns, applicable discounts, VAT (if configured), and total due.
•	Invoice status: Paid, Pending, Overdue, Cancelled
•	Export individual or bulk invoices in PDF or CSV format.

________________________________________
2. External Payment Method Management & Invoice Integration


•	Patients are required to make payments externally using third-party methods as specified on the issued invoice.
•	The Clinic Owner configures and manages accepted payment methods during clinic setup and can update them in this section.
•	Available payment method categories include:

a.	Insurance-Based Payments

o	National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF)
o	Private Health Insurers (e.g., Jubilee, Britam, APA)

b.	Digital & Mobile Payments

o	M-Pesa (via paybill or till number)
o	PesaLink (interbank transfers)
o	Bank Transfers (to clinic's designated bank account)

c.	Card Payments

o	Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
o	Point-of-Sale (POS) Machines for in-clinic payments

d.	Cash Payments

o	Physical Cash at the reception or billing desk
o	Cash Deposits into clinic bank accounts (referenced by receipt)

e.	Corporate & Employer-Based Payments

o	Company-Sponsored Medical Plans
o	Corporate Billing Agreements

f.	Government & NGO-Supported Payments

o	Public Health Subsidies
o	NGO-Funded Programs
•	Clinic Owners can add, remove, or edit accepted payment methods directly from this section.
•	All changes to payment method settings require MFA authentication via email confirmation to ensure security.
•	The selected methods will appear clearly on all generated invoices with corresponding instructions (e.g., paybill number, bank account, or corporate claim procedure).
•	Once payment is received externally, the Clinic Owner or designated Receptionist manually marks the invoice as ‘Paid’, entering payment reference, amount, and date for auditing. ________________________________________


3. Curis Platform Service Charge Management & Settlement

•	Every invoice generated by the Clinic Owner or their associated staff (Doctors, Receptionists/Clinic Assistants) automatically includes the Curis Platform service fee (e.g., 20%). This fee is transparently applied across all clinic-billed services.
•	The Super Administrator user determines the service fee format, whether as a fixed amount or a percentage, along with the specific fee figures.

Key Features in This Section:

•	Real-Time Earnings Split:
Instantly compute and display:
o	Clinic’s Net Income (after Curis deductions)
o	Amount Payable to Curis (platform service fee portion)
•	Service Fee Summary Dashboard:
Organized based on the selected payment frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly):
o	Total outstanding amounts (inclusive of past period’s outstanding amounts)
o	Total platform fees deducted over the period
o	Status of service charge invoices: Paid, Pending, or Overdue
o	Last period’s payment (only viewable based on the active payment cycle)
•	Service Charge Payment Processing:
o	Clinic Owners can make full or partial payments toward their service charge invoices directly on this page
o	Supported methods:

i.	M-Pesa (with Paybill number details)

•	Upon clicking the M-Pesa icon, a payment options pop-up is triggered showing:
o	Total Outstanding Balance
o	Action buttons:
	[Full Payment] – directs user to the Transaction Confirmation Page
	[Partial Payment] – prompts user to input desired payment amount, then proceeds to Transaction Confirmation Page
Transaction Confirmation Flow:
•	After selecting payment amount, user is prompted to choose:
o	Automatic M-Pesa Payment
o	Manual M-Pesa Payment
________________________________________
1)	If Automatic M-Pesa Payment is Selected:

•	A new pop-up appears prompting the user to:
o	Use default mobile phone number from their user profile, or
o	Enter a custom M-Pesa mobile number
•	Once a number is selected/entered, the platform sends an STK Push to the user’s mobile device.
•	Upon completion, the system automatically reconciles the transaction and proceeds to:
o	"Payment Successful" Page
o	Automatic Receipt Generation for:
	Clinic Owner
	All related staff accounts (Doctor, Receptionist/Clinic Assistant)
	Super Administrator account
________________________________________
2)	If Manual M-Pesa Payment is Selected:

•	A new pop-up displays:
o	Paybill Number
o	Account Number (auto-generated and unique per transaction)
o	Amount to Pay
•	The user is required to make the payment manually via M-Pesa.
•	The platform then monitors and automatically reconciles the payment in the background.
•	Upon confirmation:
o	User is shown the "Payment Successful" Page
o	A Receipt is Automatically Generated and shared with:
	Clinic Owner
	All associated staff accounts
	Super Administrator


ii.	 (Future-ready for: PesaLink, PayPal, Card/Bank Integrations)

•	Automated Status Updates:
o	Upon payment, the system auto-updates the invoice status to ‘Paid’, capturing:
	Payment method
	Amount paid
	Timestamp
	Reference/transaction code

This section ensures clinic owners have clear visibility and control over their financial obligations to Curis, empowering them to maintain compliance and financial clarity.
________________________________________
4. Financial Performance Dashboard


•	Display key financial KPIs such as:
o	Total revenue earned
o	Total outstanding payments
o	Income after Curis deductions
o	Most profitable services
•	Filter data by date range, doctor, or service type.
________________________________________
5. Outstanding Balance Management


•	Highlight patients with unpaid or overdue invoices.
•	Allow owners to:
o	Send payment reminders via SMS/WhatsApp/email/in-app.
o	Manually mark as paid if paid offline.
o	View payment attempt history for audit purposes.
________________________________________
6. Payment Processing History & Audit Trail


•	Detailed transaction log with:
o	Date/time
o	Invoice ID
o	Payer info
o	Amount
o	Payment status
o	Payment method
•	Use logs to verify disputed payments or failed transactions.
________________________________________
7. Receipt Issuance


•	Auto-generate downloadable receipts for completed payments.
•	Allow patients to receive receipts via email, WhatsApp or SMS (if patient contact is linked).
•	Clinic Owners can reissue receipts from the billing history section.

________________________________________
Value Delivered:

•	Zero revenue leakage: all services are captured and billed with timestamped logs.
•	Total financial transparency: clearly separates clinic revenue from Curis fees.
•	Operational efficiency: automatic invoicing and real-time payment syncing reduce manual work.
•	Audit-readiness: detailed logs and exportable reports for accounting or tax compliance.
