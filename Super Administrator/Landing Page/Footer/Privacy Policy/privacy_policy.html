Privacy Policy for Curis Super Administrator

1. Overview

Effective Date: January 11th, 2025

Last Updated: March 21st, 2025

Owner: Citrus Labs Limited

Welcome to Curis. This Privacy Policy explains how Citrus Labs Limited collects, uses, shares, and protects your data as a Super Administrator user of the Curis platform. We are fully committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya).

This policy applies to all Super Administrator activities within Curis across all partnered clinics, systems, and operations. It outlines your rights, responsibilities, and our obligations regarding data handling.

2. Data Collection

We collect:

  • Administrator Personal Data: Names, contact details, ID numbers.
  • Clinic-Level Metadata: Staff roles, permissions, configurations.
  • System-Generated Data: Login times, activity logs, device data, cookies.

Collection methods include form entries, automated tools, cookies, and direct integrations.

3. Purpose of Collection

Your data is collected strictly to:

  • Manage platform-wide permissions and settings.
  • Administer clinics, staff, and infrastructure.
  • Comply with legal requirements.
  • Improve service delivery and support.

4. Consent Management

Super Administrators consent to data collection during onboarding. You can manage, modify, or withdraw your consent directly through the Consent Management Panel. Withdrawal may affect your system access.

5. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Service Providers (e.g., cloud storage, analytics tools).
  • Legal Authorities when required by law.
  • Other Clinics only if linked via Curis and governed by agreements.

All third parties are contractually obligated to adhere to strict data protection standards.

6. Data Storage & Security

We apply the highest standards of protection, including:

  • End-to-end encryption
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Access control
  • Regular system audits
  • Staff confidentiality training

Data is retained as long as your Super Admin account remains active or for statutory periods where required.

7. Your Rights

As a Super Administrator, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Object to certain data uses.
  • Request data transfer in machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

Exercise your rights via the Privacy Rights Panel or by submitting a formal request.

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Authentication
  • Usage analytics
  • System performance

You can manage preferences through the Cookie Preferences Panel.

9. International Transfers

If data is transferred outside Kenya, we ensure it is protected under contracts or approved legal mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Policy Updates

You will be notified of all updates via in-app banners and update logs. Version history is accessible for transparency.

11. Contact Us

Questions? Concerns? Contact our Data Protection Officer.

Stay informed. Stay secure. Curis protects your data so you can lead with confidence.
