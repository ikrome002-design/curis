#### 1. **Compliance Hub**




The **Compliance Hub** page is a critical area within the **Super Administrator's** account, ensuring that the platform remains secure, compliant with relevant standards, and protected from unauthorized access. Here’s an expanded view of its dynamic features and functionalities:

### **a. Audit Log Access**
- **Comprehensive Log Tracking**:
  - The Super Admin can access and review detailed audit logs that record every user activity on the platform, including:
    - **Login attempts** (successful and failed).
    - **Actions taken** (e.g., account creation, updates, deletions).
    - **Data access** (e.g., medical records, appointment adjustments, billing changes).
    - **Changes in user roles** and permissions.
    - **IP addresses** associated with each action.
    - **Session histories** that provide timestamps of login and logout times.
    
- **Search and Filter Logs**:
  - Logs can be searched and filtered by:
    - **User type** (Patient, Doctor, Receptionist, Clinic Owner).
    - **Action type** (logins, data changes, administrative actions).
    - **Date range** to examine user activity over a specific period.
    - **IP address** or device type, if suspicious activity is detected.

- **Log Exporting**:
  - Audit logs can be exported for offline analysis, backup, or compliance reporting. They can be exported in formats such as CSV or PDF, which are ideal for generating detailed reports.

### **b. Login Attempt Monitoring**
- **Login Attempt Limits**:
  - The Super Admin can configure the number of **failed login attempts** allowed before locking an account. For example:
    - **Max failed attempts**: 5 failed login attempts before the account is locked for a specific period (e.g., 30 minutes).
    - **Customizable lockout duration** to prevent brute-force attacks.

- **Automated Locking and Alerts**:
  - When the set limit is reached, the system will **automatically lock** the account to protect it from unauthorized access.
  - A **notification** is sent to the Super Admin and the affected user, alerting them of the failed login attempts and account lock.

- **Captcha Integration**:
  - After multiple failed login attempts, a **Captcha verification** can be required to prevent automated login attempts and ensure security.
