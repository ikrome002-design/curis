### **Super Admin (Platform-Level Admin - for SaaS)**


---

### **Super Administrator – Core MVP Features**

#### 1. **Dashboard**

---

### **a. Real-Time User Overview**
- **Live counts** of Patients, Doctors, Receptionists/Clinic Assistants, and Clinic Owners.
- **Quick status indicators**: Number of active, suspended, or pending accounts.
- **Shortcut buttons** to *Add New User* or *Manage Roles*.

**Dynamic:** Auto-updates when new users are created, edited, suspended, or deleted.

---

### **b. Appointment Activity Monitoring**
- **Snapshot view**: Total appointments today, upcoming, canceled, and completed.
- **Alert widget**: List of *force-canceled appointments* requiring attention or explanation.
- **Doctor availability** status feed (who is online, offline, unavailable).

**Dynamic:** Instantly refreshes with real-time appointment changes.

---

### **c. Financial Snapshot**
- **Revenue Widget:** Total service charges collected for the week, bi-week, or month.
- **Outstanding Balances:** Top 5 Clinic Owners who owe the most.
- **Quick Report Buttons:** "View Full Financial Report" or "Configure Service Charges".

**Dynamic:** Pulls live financial data and flags any accounts with overdue payments.

---

### **d. Platform Health and Settings Status**
- **System Status:** 
  - App running normally or under maintenance (toggle indicators).
  - Session timeout settings summary.
- **Third-Party Integrations Status:**  
  - SMS/Email Gateway live or offline indicators.

**Dynamic:** Pulls latest settings and integration status.

---

### **e. Security and Compliance Alerts**
- **Security Log Summary:**
  - Latest login attempts (successful/failed).
  - Latest IP addresses accessed.
- **Suspicious Activity Warnings:**
  - Brute-force attack attempts.
  - Unusual session durations or abnormal usage patterns.

**Dynamic:** Continuously updated from backend security monitoring systems.

---

### **f. Support and Feedback Quick View**
- **Open Tickets Overview:** 
  - Total number of open support tickets.
  - Newest tickets from Patients, Doctors, Receptionists, and Clinic Owners.
- **Priority Flag:** High-priority tickets flagged red.

**Dynamic:** Live feed showing newly submitted tickets and status changes.

---

### **g. Quick Actions Panel**
- **Buttons for Immediate Admin Actions:**
  - "Add New User"
  - "View Appointments"
  - "Adjust Service Charges"
  - "View Support Tickets"
  - "Enter Maintenance Mode"

**Dynamic:** Tailored based on most urgent platform needs.

---

### **Visual Layout (Firm Recommendation):**
- Use **dashboard cards** and **live charts** (bar charts, pie charts, counters).
- **Real-time push updates** (WebSocket or polling) to avoid manual page refresh.
- **Alert banners** at the top for critical issues (e.g., "Email Gateway Down", "Security Breach Attempt Detected").
