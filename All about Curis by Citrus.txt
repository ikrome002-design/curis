**Curis** is a modern healthcare management platform designed to simplify how clinics and patients connect. It enables clinics to manage appointments, medical records, prescriptions, billing, and secure communication with patients. Patients can easily book consultations, view prescriptions, access their medical history, and handle payments—all in one place. Curis earns revenue by adding service charges to invoices generated by clinics, making it a streamlined, scalable solution for healthcare providers and patients alike.


The **Super Administrator** in the **Curis** platform is the highest-level user who manages and oversees the entire system. They are responsible for setting up and controlling platform-wide configurations, including clinic onboarding, user account management, service charge settings (fixed or percentage-based), billing and payments oversight, platform analytics, and resolving disputes. The Super Administrator ensures smooth platform operation, enforces policies, and has full authority to adjust financial settings and user permissions across all clinic accounts.

Here’s a clear and concise description of each user in the **Curis by Citrus** platform:

---

### **1. Clinic Owner**
The **Clinic Owner** is the main administrator of a clinic's account on Curis.  
- Registers the clinic and manages its operations within the platform.  
- Can create, assign, or remove **Doctor** and **Receptionist/Clinic Assistant** accounts.  
- Oversees appointments, billing, service charges, and patient interactions.  
- Responsible for choosing and settling service charge payment frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).  
- Has access to reports, financial dashboards, and high-level clinic data.

---

### **2. Doctor**
The **Doctor** provides direct healthcare services under a specific clinic.  
- Can view, manage, and update patient medical records.  
- Issues prescriptions and diagnoses.  
- Participates in consultations scheduled by patients or receptionists. Online consultations are handled via Google Meet.  
- Cannot operate independently—must be added by a **Clinic Owner**.

---

### **3. Receptionist/Clinic Assistant**
The **Receptionist/Clinic Assistant** supports clinic operations on behalf of the Clinic Owner.  
- Manages patient bookings, confirms or cancels appointments.  
- Handles check-ins, sends reminders, and supports basic administrative tasks.  
- Has limited access to patient information—no access to sensitive medical records or clinical notes.  
- Operates under the supervision of the **Clinic Owner**.

---

### **4. Patient**
The **Patient** is the individual receiving healthcare through the Curis platform.  
- Can create an account, book appointments, and consult with doctors.  
- Views personal medical history, prescriptions, and billing invoices.  
- Communicates securely with clinic staff.  
- Does not have access to other users' data or clinic-level settings.

---
