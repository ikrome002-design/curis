#### 1. **Billings & Payments**




The “Billings & Payments” page within the Patient’s Curis by Citrus web app account is designed as a dynamic, centralized interface that balances offline payment workflows with real-time visibility, family-based financial tracking, and clinic-patient communication. Below is a detailed breakdown of its MVP features and functionalities, focusing on dynamic behaviors, user flows, and interactivity.

a.	Invoice Overview – Real-Time Display and Interaction
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Invoice Feed: A live-rendered list of all outstanding and historical invoices tied to the patient and dependents.
•	Filters and Search: Patients can dynamically filter the list by:
o	Self vs. specific dependent
o	Clinic name
o	Invoice number
o	Date range
•	Invoice Cards: Each entry displays:
o	Clinic name and logo
o	Invoice number and issue date
o	Amount due or status (e.g., Paid, Pending Review, Rejected)
o	Download button (PDF generation on click)
Behavior:
•	Invoices are pushed to the page in real time once generated by the clinic.
•	Clicking “Download” immediately fetches a branded PDF invoice from the backend, usable for offline payment or insurance.
b.	Linked Accounts & Family Billing – Contextual Switching and Grouping
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Profile Switcher: Toggle between the main patient and dependents to view their specific billing history.
•	Group Invoice Display: Option to view grouped family bills per clinic or time period.
•	Payment Summary: Real-time rendering of total outstanding and paid amounts per individual or entire family.
Behavior:
•	When a parent selects a child, the list auto-filters to that child’s invoices.
•	If grouped view is enabled, invoices with the same due date or clinic can be bundled (useful for mass submissions or insurance processing).
c.	Offline Payment Processing & Proof Upload – Interactive Payment Submission
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Proof Submission Form:
o	Upload photo/screenshot (drag-and-drop or select file)
o	Enter transaction code manually (e.g., M-Pesa ID)
o	Add notes (optional)
•	“Submit for Review” Button:
o	Triggers status change to “Under Review”
o	Locks that invoice from further edits
•	Status Tracker: Displays real-time status updates:
o	Under Review
o	Verified – Paid (automatically triggers receipt and invoice removal)
o	Rejected – Resubmit Required (with reason from clinic)
Behavior:
•	Once a clinic user (Receptionist or Owner) reviews and marks payment as “Paid,”:
o	Invoice is removed from the patient view.
o	Receipt auto-sent via WhatsApp, SMS, and email.
•	If rejected, system shows rejection reason and allows new proof upload.
d.	Receipts & Invoice Communications – Multi-Channel Automation
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Real-Time Notifications:
o	Push alert on invoice generation
o	SMS/WhatsApp with invoice details and download link
•	Receipt Delivery:
o	Triggered only after the clinic marks invoice as “Paid”
o	Auto-sent via email and WhatsApp as a PDF
Behavior:
•	All notifications are triggered from backend events (invoice creation, payment confirmation).
•	Patients receive multiple alerts to prevent missed payments or unacknowledged receipts.
e.	Payment History & Tracking – Ledger-Style Review
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Timeline View: Chronological record of all payments, with details like:
o	Payment method
o	Dependent (if applicable)
o	Clinic name and service description
•	Status Filters: View only Paid, Rejected, or Pending items
Behavior:
•	Automatically updated upon clinic-side changes to payment status.
•	Ensures transparency across all past transactions.
f.	Payment Notifications & Alerts – Intelligent Reminders
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Notification Engine: Sends alerts when:
o	A new invoice is created
o	A payment is marked “Not Paid”
o	A submission is accepted or rejected
•	Customizable Alert Settings: Patients can set preferences (e.g., SMS only, no WhatsApp)
Behavior:
•	Integrates with the profile’s notification settings for family management.
•	Keeps dependents’ billing alerts within the control of the parent account holder.
g.	Support & Disputes – Resolution Workflow
Dynamic Functionalities:
•	Dispute Button:
o	Available for each invoice
o	Opens a mini-form with reason, optional file upload, and message field
•	Message History:
o	Inline communication thread between patient and clinic billing department
Behavior:
•	Status of invoice is set to “Disputed – Under Investigation”
•	Notifications are sent to both clinic staff and patient when responses are made


Summary: Dynamic MVP Strengths

Feature Area  ----  Dynamic Capability

Invoice Handling  ----  Real-time syncing, searchable list, download-on-demand
Family Account Billing  ----  Switch between users, aggregate payment summaries
Offline Payments  ----  Upload proof, track status changes, responsive feedback loop
Notifications & Alerts  ----  Multi-channel, event-driven messaging (WhatsApp, SMS, Email, In-App)
Dispute & Support  ----  Triggered workflows for resolution and direct clinic-patient communication
Data Synchronization  ----  Instant updates when clinic staff mark invoices as paid or disputed


This setup ensures patients stay informed, empowered, and able to manage healthcare payments for themselves and their families, even in an offline-first environment—all while staying in sync with clinical operations.
