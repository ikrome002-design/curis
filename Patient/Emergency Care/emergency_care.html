Health Advisory Details

Cholera Outbreak - Nairobi County

Issued: October 1, 2025

Situation Overview

Multiple cases of cholera have been confirmed in Nairobi County. The Ministry of Health is working closely with county health departments to contain the outbreak.

Recommended Safety Measures
  • Drink only boiled or bottled water
  • Wash hands frequently with soap and clean water
  • Eat thoroughly cooked food
  • Avoid raw vegetables unless properly washed
  • Practice good hygiene and sanitation
Symptoms to Watch For
  • Severe watery diarrhea
  • Vomiting
  • Rapid dehydration
  • Muscle cramps
When to Seek Immediate Medical Attention

If you or a family member experience severe diarrhea and vomiting, seek immediate medical attention at the nearest health facility.
