#### 1. **Emergency Care**




Based on the outlined system architecture and MVP goals of the Curis by Citrus web app, the "Emergency Care" page within the Patient Account should serve as a critical, life-saving access point designed for high-urgency situations. It must blend simplicity, speed, and privacy while supporting both individual patients and families with dependents.
Here’s an expounded breakdown of the dynamic MVP features and functionalities of the Emergency Care page:
________________________________________
Emergency Care – MVP Features & Functionalities

a.	National Emergency Services Directory
A curated list of emergency service contacts in Kenya with clearly labeled categories:
•	Ambulance Services (e.g., St. John, Red Cross)
•	Police & Security Hotlines
•	Fire & Rescue
•	Poison Control Centers
•	Mental Health Crisis Lines
•	Child Protection & Welfare Agencies
•	Gender-Based Violence Support Lines
Key Functional Elements:
•	Each contact has:
o	Service name
o	Phone number (text + copy-to-clipboard button)
o	Service hours (if not 24/7)
o	Location relevance (national or regional)
•	Copy-to-Clipboard Call Button
o	As Curis is a web app (not native mobile), pressing a call button triggers a “Number Copied” toast notification, so users can immediately paste it into their phone dialer.
________________________________________
b.	Personal Emergency Contact Management
A dedicated section for patients to store and manage their own trusted emergency contacts:
Editable Fields per Contact:
•	Name
•	Relationship (e.g., mother, spouse, guardian)
•	Contact number (mobile or landline)
•	Type (e.g., Next of Kin, Private Ambulance, Family Doctor)
Functional Features:
•	Add New Contact
•	Edit Existing Contact
•	Delete Contact
•	Copy-to-call action (as above)
Data is securely stored under the patient’s profile and accessible by them only, ensuring full patient data ownership.
________________________________________
c.	Dependent Emergency Support
For parents or guardians managing underage dependents:
Features Include:
•	Fast Access to Child's Emergency Info
o	Each dependent displays their:
	Name & age
	Allergies or critical conditions (optional)
	Pre-selected emergency contact (e.g., pediatrician)
•	Priority Scheduling for Dependents
o	A shortcut to Book Urgent Appointment for a child that:
	Auto-filters for pediatricians or previously visited doctors
	Highlights the soonest available time slots
	Flags the booking as Urgent in the clinic dashboard
________________________________________
d.	Urgent Clinic Communication & Alerts
Provides secure patient-to-clinic and clinic-to-patient emergency communication:
Features:
•	“Contact My Clinic” Button
o	Opens a form where the patient can:
	Select the clinic
	Describe the emergency
	Submit request (sent to clinic’s support inbox with URGENT flag)
•	Real-Time Emergency Notifications from Clinics
o	Clinics can push emergency alerts (e.g., outbreak alerts, urgent health notices)
o	Delivered to patients via:
	SMS
	WhatsApp
	In-app push notification
________________________________________
e.	Emergency Location Quick Reference
If patients have multiple dependents or live in rural areas:
•	Integration with geographical filters for emergency contact numbers per county or region (e.g., Nairobi Ambulance vs. Mombasa Fire Department)
________________________________________
Security & Access Rules
•	Only logged-in patients can manage their personal contacts.
•	Parents can only see/manage emergency data for their dependents under 18.
•	No access to another adult’s emergency data even within the same family account.
•	All data encrypted and stored securely, following same security protocols as medical records.

Family-Centered Urgent Support  -----  Fast-track pediatric appointment scheduling and alerts
