#### 1. **My Medical Cover**




The "My Medical Cover" page in the Curis by Citrus patient web app is a dynamic MVP module designed to streamline insurance claim submissions, enable multi-user family-based processing, and ensure automated, accurate medical documentation. Below is a clear breakdown of its dynamic functionalities and operational flow:
________________________________________
Dynamic MVP Features of the "My Medical Cover" Page

a.	Family-Based Claim Submission Engine
•	Multi-User Invoice Generation
o	Patients can generate insurance-ready invoices not just for themselves but also for each linked dependent.
o	Each invoice clearly indicates the patient/dependent name, associated clinic, service details, and attending doctor.
•	Switch User Context
o	Users can dynamically switch between themselves and each dependent while preparing claims. The interface adapts to the selected user, updating medical history and billing data in real time.
•	Claim Type Selection
o	Choose between:
	Outpatient
	Inpatient
	Specialist Consultation
	Lab Tests/Diagnostics
	Medication
o	Based on the type, the form auto-adjusts to require the relevant insurance fields and document attachments.
________________________________________
b.	Integrated Insurance Provider Directory
•	Selectable Insurance Carriers List
o	Dropdown menu of supported insurance providers fetched dynamically from the Curis system's master list.
o	Each provider profile includes:
	Name
	Claim submission email/contact
	Service categories covered
	Custom claim instructions or restrictions
•	Auto-fill Capabilities
o	Once a provider is selected, relevant claim submission guidelines are displayed.
o	The platform auto-fills known patient policy details if previously saved.
________________________________________
c.	Auto-Attachment of Medical Records
•	Smart Document Aggregator
o	For each selected invoice, the platform automatically attaches supporting medical documentation, such as:
	Consultation summary
	Doctor notes
	Lab results
	Prescriptions
	Clinic name & doctor credentials
•	Document Review Preview
o	Patients can preview all attached documents before claim submission.
o	Option to add external files manually (e.g., referral forms or doctor letters).
________________________________________
d.	Claim Submission Tracking & Status
•	Submission Dashboard
o	Displays list of submitted claims with:
	Patient name
	Dependent (if applicable)
	Insurance provider
	Claim amount
	Date of submission
________________________________________
e.	Invoice Validation for Insurance
•	Verification Checks
o	Only marked-as-paid invoices can be submitted for insurance.
o	If a patient attempts to claim using an unpaid invoice, the system blocks submission and prompts for proof of payment.
•	Insurance-Grade Invoice Format
o	Downloadable and shareable PDF invoices with:
	Official clinic header
	Patient details
	Service breakdown
	Amount paid
	Invoice number
	Doctor signature (optional)
________________________________________
f.	Dependent Coverage Logic
•	Policy Number Mapping by Dependent
o	Each dependent can have their own insurance provider and policy number.
o	The system allows patients to save these under their profile in Profile & Settings > Dependents Info.
•	Coverage Eligibility Indicator
o	Visual indicator if a dependent’s insurance info is missing or if a service is not covered under their policy (based on preset clinic rules).
________________________________________
Security and Compliance Controls


•	Only the primary patient (adult) can initiate and submit claims for themselves or their dependents.
•	Claims for dependents aged 13–17 will omit sensitive details in the bundled documentation, per privacy settings.
•	All submitted claim documents are encrypted and securely stored in the patient’s record for auditing and resubmission if needed.
________________________________________
Summary: Why "My Medical Cover" Is a Dynamic MVP Feature



Feature  -----  Dynamic Functionality


Multi-user claim creation  -----  Supports dependent-specific insurance claims
Integrated provider directory  -----  Auto-fills, filters, and validation
Smart document bundling  -----  Automatically attaches relevant health records
Submission & tracking  -----  Status updates, alerts, and history logs
Invoice eligibility checks  -----  Prevents unpaid invoice claims
Security & age-based logic  -----  Controlled access and data presentation


________________________________________


This design ensures that patients on Curis can handle insurance claims easily, securely, and efficiently, with minimal back-and-forth between clinics and insurers, all while maintaining compliance with privacy and age-based access control.
